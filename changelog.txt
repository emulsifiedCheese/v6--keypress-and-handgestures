added keypress and hand gestures to apply filters

current :
    Press spacebar to take a snapshot, ESC to clear snapshot
    Press 1 and touch thumb to index to greyscale image
    Touch thumb to index to pixelate image
    Touch thumb to pinkie to blur image
    Hold palm out to apply HSV

    Press 1 or touch thumb to index to greyscale image
    Press 2 or touch thumb to index to pixelate image
    Press 3 or ouch thumb to pinkie to blur image
    Press 4 or hold palm out to apply HSV
